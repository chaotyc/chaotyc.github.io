## Review checklist

1. Confirm every sentence and figure is public-safe.
2. Confirm every image is original, licensed, or explicitly approved.
3. Label simulated and projected results clearly.
4. Verify collaborator and advisor credit before adding names.
5. Obtain research-group or employer review where appropriate.
